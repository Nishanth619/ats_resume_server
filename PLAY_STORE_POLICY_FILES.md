# Play Store Policy Files

This file is the master index for the Google Play Store policy documents prepared for ATS Resume Builder.

## Required Public Pages

- Privacy Policy: `legal/privacy-policy.md`
- Terms and Conditions: `legal/terms-and-conditions.md`
- Account Deletion Page: `legal/account-deletion.md`

## Play Console Helper Files

- Google Play Data Safety Notes: `legal/google-play-data-safety-notes.md`
- Google Play Compliance Review: `legal/google-play-compliance-review.md`

## Recommended Hosted URLs

Use these URLs in the app and Google Play Console after hosting the pages:

- Privacy Policy: `https://ats-resume-builder.app/privacy`
- Terms and Conditions: `https://ats-resume-builder.app/terms`
- Account Deletion: `https://ats-resume-builder.app/account-deletion`

## Before Publishing

Complete these checks before submitting to Google Play:

1. Replace the operator/developer name with the exact Google Play developer or company name.
2. Host the Privacy Policy as a public, non-PDF web page.
3. Host the Account Deletion page as a public web page.
4. Verify the in-app account deletion option in Settings works in production.
5. Verify the in-app report/flag option for AI-generated outputs works in production.
6. Fill the Play Console Data safety form using `legal/google-play-data-safety-notes.md`.
7. Confirm ads are declared because the app uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.
8. Keep paid feature/subscription claims disabled until Play Billing or RevenueCat is fully implemented.
