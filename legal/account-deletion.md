# Account Deletion

Effective date: May 10, 2026

Use this page as the public account deletion page for Google Play Console:

https://ats-resume-builder.app/account-deletion

Before publishing, host this page at the URL above or update the app and Play Console to the final hosted URL.

## Delete Your ATS Resume Builder Account

If you want to delete your ATS Resume Builder account and associated app data, email:

support@ats-resume-builder.app

Use the subject line:

Account Deletion Request

In your email, include the email address used for your ATS Resume Builder account. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting the account.

## What Will Be Deleted

When your deletion request is verified, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your app account, including:

- Account profile information
- Saved resumes and resume versions
- Saved cover letters
- Job tracker entries
- Uploaded profile photos and generated resume PDFs stored by the app
- App-specific usage records tied to your account where deletion is technically supported

## What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained if required or permitted for legal, security, fraud prevention, billing, tax, dispute-resolution, or compliance reasons. Examples may include purchase records handled by Google Play or subscription providers, security logs, support records, and records required to comply with law.

## Timing

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.
