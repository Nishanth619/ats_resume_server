# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ATS Resume Builder ("ATS Resume Builder," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information when you use the ATS Resume Builder mobile app, website, backend services, and related features (together, the "Service").

Privacy contact: support@ats-resume-builder.app

Before publishing, make sure the developer name shown in your Google Play store listing matches the developer/operator name in this policy, or update this policy to include that exact legal name.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information created while using the Service, and information collected automatically by our app and service providers.

### Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Firebase user ID and authentication tokens
- Sign-in provider information, including Google Sign-In if you choose to use it
- Account plan, subscription status, account creation date, and last active date

### Resume, profile, and job-search content

The app is designed to help you create resumes, cover letters, ATS checks, job applications, and related job-search materials. Depending on the features you use, we may collect and store:

- Resume titles, templates, color preferences, sections, version history, target roles, target job descriptions, ATS scores, and download counts
- Personal resume details you enter, such as name, email, phone number, location, LinkedIn/profile links, summary, skills, education, experience, projects, certifications, awards, and languages
- Cover letters, company names, generated text, word counts, and related metadata
- Job tracker information, including companies, roles, application status, notes, and applied dates
- Profile photos or other images you choose to upload
- PDF or DOCX files generated or uploaded through the Service
- LinkedIn profile or LinkedIn export data if you choose to import it

Please do not include sensitive information in your resume or job-search content unless it is necessary for your own use of the Service.

### Files, photos, and device permissions

The app may request permission to access photos, media, or files only when needed for user-selected features such as uploading a profile photo, importing a LinkedIn ZIP export, or saving/sharing a resume file. The app does not access your files or photos in the background.

### AI feature inputs and outputs

When you use AI features such as ATS analysis, keyword matching, bullet improvement, resume tailoring, summary generation, or cover letter generation, we process the text you submit and the AI-generated output. This may include resume text, job descriptions, company names, target roles, and other content you provide.

### Usage, diagnostics, and device information

We and our service providers may collect:

- App interactions, feature usage, and usage limits
- Crash logs, diagnostics, app performance data, and error reports
- Device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate region, and network-related information
- Advertising identifiers or similar device identifiers when ads are shown
- Local preferences stored on your device, such as theme setting, onboarding state, and local usage counters

### Payment and subscription information

If paid features are enabled, purchases are processed by Google Play and/or subscription service providers such as RevenueCat. We do not collect or store full payment card numbers. We may receive and store subscription status, product identifiers, purchase/entitlement status, renewal state, and related transaction metadata needed to provide paid features and support purchases.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, and manage your account
- Save, sync, display, edit, export, and delete resumes, cover letters, profile data, and job tracker entries
- Generate ATS scores, summaries, tailored resumes, improved bullets, keyword matches, and cover letters
- Import user-selected LinkedIn data or uploaded files
- Generate and share resume files or share links when you choose to use those features
- Provide free, ad-supported, and paid features
- Show ads, measure ad performance, prevent abuse, and comply with advertising rules
- Monitor app reliability, fix bugs, improve performance, and maintain security
- Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and policy violations
- Respond to support requests, legal requests, and user deletion requests
- Comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies

We do not sell your personal and sensitive user data.

## 3. AI Processing and Content Responsibility

The Service uses AI to assist with resume and career-document writing. AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate. You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and verifying all AI-generated content before using it in job applications or sharing it with others.

We use safeguards and input validation to reduce unsafe or unsupported outputs, but no AI system is perfect. You can report offensive, unsafe, misleading, or otherwise problematic AI output by contacting support@ats-resume-builder.app. The app should also provide an in-app reporting option for AI-generated output before Google Play production release.

## 4. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to operate, secure, improve, monetize, and support the Service, or when you direct us to share it.

### Service providers

We may share information with service providers that process information for us, including:

- Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics
- Google Sign-In
- Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
- Google Play Billing and Google Play services
- RevenueCat, if subscription management is enabled
- Backend hosting and infrastructure providers, including Render or equivalent hosting providers
- AI providers used by the backend, including Google Gemini and Groq, when AI features are used
- LinkedIn, when you choose LinkedIn OAuth or LinkedIn import features

These providers may process information according to their own privacy terms and data processing practices.

### User-directed sharing

If you choose to export, download, open, or share a resume, cover letter, PDF, DOCX, share link, or other file, the information may be shared with the apps, people, websites, or services you select. We are not responsible for how third parties handle information after you choose to share it.

### Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, our rights, app security, or the integrity of the Service.

### Business transfers

If the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction with appropriate notice where required by law.

## 5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including transmission over HTTPS, Firebase security rules, authentication, access controls, and backend validation. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for reviewing the information you choose to add to resumes, cover letters, and shared files.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce our terms.

In general:

- Account records are kept while your account remains active.
- Resume, cover letter, job tracker, profile, and file data are kept until you delete them or request account deletion.
- Share links are intended to expire after the period shown in the app or backend response, such as 30 days.
- Crash logs, diagnostics, analytics, ad data, and security logs may be retained for limited periods according to our providers' settings and operational needs.
- Some records may be retained longer if required for legal, tax, security, fraud prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons.

## 7. Deleting Your Information and Account

You may delete individual resumes, applications, and some generated content in the app where deletion controls are available.

To request deletion of your account and associated app data, use the account deletion page at:

https://ats-resume-builder.app/account-deletion

You may also contact support@ats-resume-builder.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request." We may need to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

When we delete an app account based on your request, we will delete or anonymize the personal data associated with that app account unless retention is required or permitted for legal, security, fraud prevention, billing, tax, dispute-resolution, or compliance reasons. Temporary account deactivation is not treated as account deletion.

## 8. Your Choices

You can:

- Choose whether to create an account or sign in with Google
- Edit or delete resumes, cover letters, and job tracker entries where supported
- Decline optional file/photo permissions, although related features may not work
- Choose not to use LinkedIn import, AI features, ads-for-rewards features, or share-link features
- Manage ad personalization and device advertising ID settings through your device and Google account settings
- Request access, correction, or deletion by contacting support@ats-resume-builder.app

## 9. Children's Privacy

ATS Resume Builder is intended for job seekers and professional/career use. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

If you choose a target audience in Google Play that includes children, you must review and update the app, ads, SDKs, and this policy to comply with Google Play Families requirements.

## 10. International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. These countries may have data protection laws different from those in your jurisdiction. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal bases and safeguards for international processing and transfers.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above and, where required, provide additional notice. Continued use of the Service after a policy update means the updated policy applies.
